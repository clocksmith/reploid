# INTENT: Reploid

Parent: none

## Need

Humans and agents need intelligence beyond one participating device's practical
capacity without surrendering control of their data, devices, or evolving software.

## Target

A visible intelligence network whose agents combine resources and complementary
approaches to accomplish more and improve their individual and collective methods.
Independent operation remains possible; the collaborating network remains the
product. Use uncluttered workflows, brief copy, shared components
and tokens, accessible light/dark themes, responsive scrolling, and futuristic,
subtly neumorphic styling.

## Invariants

- Participation provides an otherwise unavailable capability, better results,
  greater reliability, or lower total resource cost. Each participant controls
  its contribution, disclosures, and adoption of changes, and can stop contributing.
- Conversations have independent membership, history, permissions and attempts.
  Selecting, closing or cancelling one cannot stop unrelated conversations.
  Requesting intelligence and contributing storage or execution are separate grants.
  The participant's device is part of the mesh, not a competing local product.
- Doppler owns model computation; Poolday owns peer infrastructure; Reploid owns
  orchestration and improvement.
- Weight-shard storage, whole-request inference, computational partitions, and
  agent subtasks retain distinct contracts. Custody grants no execution permission.
  Whole jobs prove no partitioning. Intermediate tensors require disclosure grants;
  placement preserves model semantics and learned expert selection.
- Budgets and permissions constrain eligibility. Declare numerical tolerances and
  cancellation settlement, not universal determinism or immediate termination.
- Belief updates, strategy changes, and weight changes are distinct.
  Preserve uncertainty, provenance, and observation dependencies; duplicated
  evidence cannot count as independent experience.
- Evolution requires independent evaluation and adoption within explicit,
  reversible operator grants. Model-weight changes require separate execution
  and evaluation contracts.
- Candidates cannot control evaluators, adoption authority, or grants.
- Runtime operation within valid grants does not depend on CI.
- Signed records do not establish correct computation.

## Evidence

Compare useful outcomes with the strongest feasible local alternative and,
separately, equivalent centralized resources. Count communication, verification,
evaluation, failures, resource limits, and human intervention. Test transfer to
unfamiliar subsequent tasks.
Recursive benefit additionally requires causal A-to-B-to-C evidence: B improves
on A and its changed machinery contributes to C.

## Non-goals

Activity, reproduction, receipts, adoption, and UI polish alone prove neither
network benefit nor recursive improvement.
