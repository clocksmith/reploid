## Retired design

This design proposed `/capabilities/cognition/tool-evaluator.js`, a tool through which the model scored its own output against a rubric during each cycle. That module does not exist. Self-evaluation designs are consolidated in the Proposed [0x00003E](0x00003E-meta-cognitive-coordination.md). A model's score of its own work informs a decision; it never grants adoption (see [INTENT.md](../../INTENT.md)).
