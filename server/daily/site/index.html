    Rules

    • Three tries per signed-in guest each UTC day.
    • Match the whole answer. Case, outside whitespace and one final punctuation mark are ignored.
    • After Unicode normalization, runs of letters and digits form words. Hyphens and apostrophes split them. Word ties use the number of characters in the trimmed prompt.
    • Write in any language. Translations, riddles and analogies are allowed. Split spellings, listed inflections and lookalikes of banned words are blocked. “I” is banned only as a whole word when listed.
    • Each turn starts fresh. Thinking is off. The pinned model gets up to 16 output tokens.
    What the circle counts

    We count opened dares, guests who play or pass on a dare, and players who return the next day. These counts exclude prompts, answers and email addresses.
