How to play

Write a prompt that makes the model give the target answer. You get 5 tries each UTC day. The shortest winning prompt tops your circle’s board.

Scoring and banned words

We compare the whole answer, ignoring case, surrounding whitespace, and one final punctuation mark.

Runs of letters or digits count as words after Unicode normalization. Hyphens and apostrophes split them. Characters break ties. Limit: 240 characters.

Translations, analogies and riddles are fair game. Split spellings, lookalikes and listed word forms are banned. A banned “I” counts only as a whole word.

Model and privacy

Every turn starts fresh with thinking off and a 16-token limit. The serving computer can read your prompt.

A dare shares your score and the recorded answer. Showing your winning prompt tomorrow requires your permission and the host’s review.

The host can count dare opens, plays and next-day returns without prompt text in those counts.
