Reploid DailyToday’s puzzle

Host your own circle

One circle plays on one GPU. You choose the daily turn budget and can revoke the circle invitation.

  1. Prepare a Reploid fleet requester and an enrolled provider for the pinned small model.
  2. Configure email sign-in and load the signed daily calendar and physical target qualifications. A target needs two solutions on Apple, AMD and your serving backend.
  3. Start the daily server and issue a circle invitation. Guests can pass the same invitation along with their score.

From a Reploid source checkout, open the staged local preview with:

npm run daily -- preview

Open the complete hosting guide for configuration, grant management and winner review.
